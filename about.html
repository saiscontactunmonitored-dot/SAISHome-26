ABOUT SAIS

Supporting animal care, information and local businesses.

Somerset Animal Information Services was created to make finding useful animal information and trusted local services easier for people across Somerset and the UK.

OUR STORY

From a small local project to a wider animal information service.

Somerset Animal Information Services was established in 2023.

What started as a small page within the Wilstock and Stockmoor Animal Friends and Rescue Support Group gradually developed into something much bigger.

As the project grew, so did the need for a dedicated place where people could find animal-related businesses, services, information and useful resources without having to search across numerous different websites and social media pages.

SAIS has continued to develop from that original idea into an independent animal information and business directory service supporting people, businesses and animal-focused organisations.

ESTABLISHED

2023

Since 2023, SAIS has continued to develop its website, directory, information resources and business network.

Today, the service connects people with animal businesses and information across Somerset and provides access to wider UK animal information.

WHY SAIS EXISTS

Making the search for animal services simpler.

Finding the right animal business or useful information should not mean spending hours searching through different websites, social media pages and directories.

SAIS brings those different elements together and gives animal owners, businesses and organisations a central place to start.

The service continues to grow through local businesses, recommended partners, useful information and people sharing SAIS with others.
