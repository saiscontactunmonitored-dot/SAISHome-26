ABOUT SAIS

Making animal information easier to find.

Somerset Animal Information Services brings together animal-related businesses, services and useful information to help people find what they need.

OUR STORY

From a small idea to a growing animal information service.

Somerset Animal Information Services was established in 2023 with a simple aim: to make it easier for people to find useful animal-related businesses, services and information.

SAIS originally began as a small page within Wilstock and Stockmoor Animal Friends and Rescue Support Group. As the idea developed, it grew beyond its original purpose and became a standalone information and directory service.

Today, SAIS provides a central starting point for people looking for animal-related services across Somerset, alongside a wider information hub covering useful animal care information from across the UK.

CLEAR FROM THE START

What SAIS is — and what it isn't.

We believe it is important to be clear about what our service does.

SAIS is

  • An animal information service
  • A directory for animal-related businesses
  • A place to discover useful animal resources
  • A platform supporting local animal businesses
  • A starting point for finding relevant services

SAIS isn't

  • A rescue centre
  • An animal welfare organisation
  • A rehoming service
  • A veterinary service
  • A replacement for qualified professional advice

OUR APPROACH

Straightforward information without the unnecessary barriers.

We want SAIS to be useful, accessible and straightforward.

That means making information easier to find, keeping the directory accessible to local businesses and providing clear routes to relevant services and resources.

SAIS is built around the idea that finding animal-related help should not have to mean searching through endless pages, social media posts or disconnected websites.

BEHIND SAIS

Built by someone who works with animals.

Darren — Founder & Owner

SAIS was founded and is managed by Darren, who created the service to make animal-related information and local services easier to find.

What started as a small project in 2023 has continued to develop into a wider directory and information platform supporting people and animal-related businesses.

SAIS remains independently run, with its direction focused on making the service genuinely useful rather than simply building another online directory.
