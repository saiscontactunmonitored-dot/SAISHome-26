ABOUT SOMERSET ANIMAL INFORMATION SERVICES

A small idea that grew into SAIS.

Established in 2023, Somerset Animal Information Services was created to provide reliable, accessible support for animal owners, carers and local animal-related businesses.

OUR STORY

From a small Facebook page to a growing platform.

Somerset Animal Information Services was established in 2023 to provide reliable, accessible support for animal owners, carers and local businesses.

What began as a small page within the Facebook group Wilstock and Stockmoor Animal Friends and Rescue Support Group — now known as Animal Friends and Rescue Support Group — quickly grew into a much larger platform.

As the idea developed, so did SAIS. The website, social media presence and online services continued to expand, creating a dedicated space for animal information, resources and local businesses.

HOW WE'VE GROWN

More than a directory.

Since launching, the website and social media pages have developed significantly, becoming a trusted space for guidance, resources and community support.

The platform has gone through many improvements over time and continues to evolve as the needs of its audience change.

Alongside animal information, SAIS is committed to helping small, local animal-related businesses grow. The platform has already supported individuals in starting their own ventures through advice, promotion and encouragement.

Darren Founder & Owner / CEO

MEET THE FOUNDER

Built from a lifelong passion for animals.

Darren Owner / CEO

Darren is the owner and creator of Somerset Animal Information Services.

At 18 years old, he works full-time as an animal care assistant at a local animal centre in Somerset, gaining hands-on experience caring for dogs and cats every day.

Outside of work and SAIS, Darren lives with four rescue dogs, two cats and several small animals.

OVERCOMING CHALLENGES

Determination matters.

Darren is open about having dyslexia and takes pride in building something meaningful through determination and hard work.

Despite the challenges he has faced, he has grown SAIS from a small Facebook page into a recognised and expanding platform.

His goal is to continue growing the platform while supporting others, particularly people who are starting their own animal-related businesses.

OUR MISSION

Why SAIS exists.

The mission is simple: to support animals, their owners and the wider community in every way possible.

SAIS brings together information, local businesses and community resources in one place, helping people find what they need while giving animal-related businesses an opportunity to be discovered.
