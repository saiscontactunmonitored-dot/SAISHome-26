ABOUT SAIS

About Somerset Animal Information Services

Supporting animal lovers, local businesses and animal-focused services across Somerset and the UK since 2023.

WHO WE ARE

A central place for animal information and local services.

Somerset Animal Information Services, commonly known as SAIS, was established in 2023 with a simple aim: to make useful animal information and animal-related services easier to find.

What began as a small page within the Animal Friends and Rescue Support Group grew into a wider online service supporting animal owners, local businesses and organisations.

Today, SAIS provides a dedicated animal business directory alongside an information hub containing useful animal-related resources and guidance.

OUR APPROACH

Independent. Practical. Built to be useful.

SAIS is not an animal rescue organisation, animal welfare charity or recruitment service.

Our role is to provide information, signposting, directory services and support for animal-related businesses and the people looking for them.

Supporting the wider animal community.

We believe useful information should be easy to find and local businesses should have an opportunity to be discovered.

By bringing these things together, SAIS aims to create a central starting point for people looking for animal-related services and information.

The service continues to develop as new businesses, resources, partnerships and ideas are added.

RECOGNITION

Award-winning service.

SAIS was recognised through the UK Enterprise Awards for its work supporting animal lovers, local businesses and the wider animal community.

The recognition reflects the development of SAIS from a small project in 2023 into an established online animal information and business service.

THE PERSON BEHIND SAIS

Built from practical experience.

SAIS was founded and developed by Darren, who continues to manage the service and its development.

Darren's background in animal care and his experience working with animals has helped shape SAIS around practical information, accessible services and support for the people caring for animals.

SAIS remains independently run, with the focus staying on making the platform useful for animal owners and animal-related businesses.
