WHY IT HELPS

Every share makes SAIS easier to find.

SAIS does not rely on people already knowing that the service exists.

Sharing the website, following the social pages and leaving an honest review all help introduce SAIS to new people.

Supporting SAIS does not have to cost you anything.

There is no requirement to donate money or buy anything.

A few seconds spent sharing a page, recommending the directory to someone or telling a local animal business about SAIS can make a genuine difference.

If you already use SAIS, simply telling someone else about it is one of the most useful things you can do.

Find something useful? Pass it on.

Whether it is the Directory, Information Hub or one of the businesses featured through SAIS, sharing the service helps keep useful animal information moving.
