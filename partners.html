Our network

Supporting animal care. Supporting businesses. Supporting SAIS.

Somerset Animal Information Services brings together animal-focused businesses, services and organisations committed to supporting animal owners and promoting responsible animal care.

Our recommended partners are part of a growing network across Somerset and the UK. From enrichment and pet care to animal support and specialist services, these are businesses and organisations SAIS is proud to feature.

Featured Partner The Muzzle Movement

Supporting Positive Muzzle Experiences

Practical muzzle products and positive muzzle education.

The Muzzle Movement focuses on practical muzzle products and positive muzzle education, supporting responsible muzzle use and helping dogs feel more comfortable and confident when wearing a muzzle.

Their approach is centred around making muzzle use a positive and practical part of life for dogs and their owners.

Rescue 2 Home Animal Support

Because adoption is not just the beginning.

Rescue 2 Home Animal Support provides practical support for animals and their owners, helping pets settle into their new homes and supporting owners throughout the journey beyond adoption.
