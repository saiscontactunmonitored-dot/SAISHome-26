THE SAIS NETWORK

Supporting animal care. Supporting businesses.

SAIS Recommended Partners are businesses, services and organisations that share a commitment to supporting animal owners and promoting responsible animal care.

Our partner network brings together different types of animal-focused services, from enrichment and pet care to practical support for animals and their owners.

We are proud to give these businesses another way to reach people looking for useful products, services and support.

WHY PARTNERS MATTER

Good businesses deserve to be found.

Finding the right service can make a real difference to an animal and the person caring for them.

Our recommended partner network helps bring trusted animal-focused services into one place.

The Muzzle Movement

Supporting Positive Muzzle Experiences

The Muzzle Movement focuses on practical muzzle products and positive muzzle education, helping dogs become more comfortable and confident when wearing a muzzle.

Their approach supports responsible muzzle use while helping owners understand how to introduce and use muzzles in a positive way.
