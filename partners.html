OUR PARTNERS

Supporting animal care.
Supporting businesses.

Somerset Animal Information Services brings together animal-focused businesses, services and organisations that provide useful support to animal owners.

Our recommended partners are part of a growing network across Somerset and the UK. We are proud to highlight businesses that provide useful products, services and support within the animal sector.

FEATURED PARTNERS

Explore our recommended network.

01
FEATURED PARTNER

The Muzzle Movement

Supporting Positive Muzzle Experiences

The Muzzle Movement focuses on practical muzzle products and positive muzzle education, helping owners make muzzle use a more comfortable and positive experience for their dogs.

Their approach supports responsible muzzle use while helping dogs feel more comfortable and confident wearing a muzzle.

Visit The Muzzle Movement
02
DOG TRAINING • BEHAVIOUR • PET CARE

Small & Mighty Pets

Friendly, reward-based support for dogs and their owners.

Small & Mighty Pets provides reward-based dog training and behaviour support for owners looking for practical, positive help.

Services include puppy training, one-to-one training, behavioural consultations and more. Based in Taunton, they support dog owners across the surrounding area.

Visit Small & Mighty Pets
03
ANIMAL SUPPORT • PET CARE

Rescue 2 Home Animal Support

Because adoption is not just the beginning.

Rescue 2 Home Animal Support provides practical support for animals and their owners, helping pets settle into their homes and supporting owners through the journey beyond adoption.

Their support can help with adjustment, new environments and the challenges that can come with bringing an animal into a new home.

Visit Rescue 2 Home
04
ENRICHMENT • SNUFFLE MATS • DOG WELLBEING

Blitz Snuffle Mats

Encouraging Enrichment Through Sniffing

Blitz Snuffle Mats creates enrichment products designed to encourage dogs to use their natural sniffing instincts.

Snuffle mats can form part of an everyday enrichment routine, helping make activities more engaging and rewarding for dogs.

Visit Blitz Snuffle Mats
