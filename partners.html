OUR NETWORK

Useful services from businesses we are happy to recommend.

The SAIS Recommended Partners network highlights businesses and services that may be useful to animal owners, carers and people looking for specialist animal-related products or support.

Each partner operates independently from Somerset Animal Information Services. We provide this page as a way of helping people discover services that may be relevant to them.

Please contact each business directly for its current services, prices, availability and individual terms.
