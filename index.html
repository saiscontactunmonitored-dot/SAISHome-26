03 / ABOUT SAIS

Started small.
Built to be useful.

Founded in 2023, Somerset Animal Information Services has grown from a small animal-information project into a wider platform for discovery, information and connection.

SAIS helps people discover animal-related businesses, services and useful information across Somerset and the UK.

SAIS is not a rescue centre, animal welfare organisation or jobs platform. It is an information and business service designed to make useful things easier to find.
