02 / ABOUT SAIS SOMERSET ANIMAL INFORMATION SERVICES

Built in Somerset.
Built around animals.

Somerset Animal Information Services began in 2023 with a simple idea:

make animal information and local connections easier to find.

Since then, SAIS has developed into a wider platform supporting the discovery of animal-related businesses, services, information and opportunities across Somerset.

SAIS is not a rescue centre, animal welfare organisation or jobs platform. It exists to help people find useful information and connect with the animal businesses and resources around them.
