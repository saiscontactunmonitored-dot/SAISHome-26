ABOUT SAIS

Making animal information easier to find.

Somerset Animal Information Services was established in 2023 to make it easier for people to find animal-related businesses, services and useful information.

SAIS is not a rescue centre or animal welfare organisation. We provide an information and directory service, helping people discover what is available locally and further afield.
