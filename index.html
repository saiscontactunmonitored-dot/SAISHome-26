ABOUT SAIS

Built to make finding animal support easier.

Somerset Animal Information Services began in 2023 and has grown into an independent online service connecting animal lovers with businesses, services and useful information.

Whether you are looking for a local business, practical animal information or somewhere to start when you need support, SAIS brings those resources together in one place.
